# Future feature candidates

These are planning notes, **not features available in WorkCraft AI today**. Do not add them to public pricing or promise a release date until they are built and verified.

## Service agreements and recurring maintenance plans

**Problem:** A contractor who sells annual HVAC tune-ups, seasonal landscaping, or other repeat service must remember the next visit, contact the customer, and bill it manually. This is different from WorkCraft AI's $9.99/month **contractor Pro subscription**: a service agreement is between the contractor and their customer.

**Recommended first release:** Let a Pro contractor create a reusable service-plan template and assign it to a customer/property. Record the agreed scope, visit frequency and due window, price-book-backed rates or fixed price, billing choice (per visit or per period), start/end date, local time zone, renewal/cancellation terms, and whether the customer has accepted. Show upcoming and overdue visits on Schedule & Jobs and the customer profile. Generate one **draft** job per due visit; the contractor confirms its actual date and time. Once scheduled, it appears in the existing calendar subscription feed. Send a bounded email reminder before a scheduled visit and a renewal reminder before expiry, subject to the customer's communication preferences. Let the contractor create or review a draft invoice after a visit, then send it through the existing payment flow. Nothing is charged automatically in this first release.

**Later billing phase:** Support contractor-controlled automatic invoice creation and, separately, optional recurring customer payment through the contractor's Stripe connected account. Require clear customer authorization for any saved payment method or recurring charge. Keep service-plan charges and receipts separate from the contractor's WorkCraft AI Pro subscription. Make per-visit and flat-period billing explicit so a visit is never charged twice. Preserve contractor control over tax, price changes, refunds, pauses, cancellations, failed payments, and renewal notices. Review the customer-facing agreement and payment authorization before enabling auto-charge.

**Implementation shape:** Add account-owned plan templates, customer agreements, visit occurrences, and billing occurrences with row-level security and audit history. Each occurrence needs a stable unique key (agreement + scheduled cycle + type) so retries cannot duplicate jobs, invoices, or reminders. A scheduled worker should create or flag upcoming occurrences, enqueue notifications, and record delivery outcomes. Handle rescheduling, daylight-saving changes, skipped visits, expired agreements, and account downgrades. A customer link should display the current scope, schedule, price, and acceptance state without exposing other customers' data. Add an Admin Support view for due/overdue visits and failed automation, not a new source of contractor pricing.

**Suggested order:** (1) templates, accepted agreements, visit tracking, and contractor reminders; (2) customer visit and renewal email; (3) reviewed draft invoices; (4) optional automatic invoicing and recurring payments after the earlier stages prove reliable. Validate the flow with HVAC and lawn-care pilot users before expanding it.

Jobber's [recurring-job guide](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/create-a-recurring-job/) distinguishes repeating visits from per-visit versus fixed-price invoicing. Housecall Pro's [service-plan dashboard](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/2932107-service-plans-dashboard-overview) tracks unscheduled visits and plans due for billing, while its [visit guide](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/3075085-schedule-service-plan-visits) links visits to jobs. These are reference workflows, not commitments to copy every feature.

## Customer Buy Now, Pay Later through Stripe

**Problem:** A homeowner may want to spread payment for a larger accepted estimate while the contractor is paid according to the eligible payment method's settlement terms.

**Possible scope:** Evaluate Stripe's eligible Buy Now, Pay Later methods on the existing connected-account Checkout for customer estimate payments. Show a method only when Stripe confirms availability for the contractor, customer, amount, location, and type of work. Keep deposits, remaining balances, refunds, disputes, and fee disclosure accurate; a split-payment plan must never be represented as WorkCraft AI lending money. Compare contractor processing fees and conversion before deciding whether to promote it. Do not assume every home-improvement or trade job qualifies.

**Release gate:** Verify method eligibility and connected-account support in a Stripe sandbox, customer-facing payment copy, payment and refund webhooks, reconciliation, and at least one legitimate live transaction. Stripe's [BNPL overview](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/buy-now-pay-later) is the primary reference.

## QuickBooks Online and Xero accounting sync

**Problem:** Contractors or office staff should not re-enter customer records, invoices, and payments in accounting software. This is a future Pro integration, not a current WorkCraft AI capability.

**Recommended ownership model:** WorkCraft AI remains the source for estimates, job scope, customer-facing invoices, and Stripe Connect customer payments. Accounting software remains the source for the contractor's chart of accounts, bookkeeping adjustments, tax filings, and bank reconciliation. “Two-way” should mean explicitly supported fields and status updates in both directions, **not** unrestricted editing of the same invoice in two systems.

**First release:** Let each contractor connect one QuickBooks Online company through OAuth. On initial setup, preview and confirm customer matches before importing or linking records. Export a customer when needed for billing; export an invoice only after the contractor sends or finalizes it; export Stripe-paid amounts as payments against the *same* accounting invoice. Reflect an external paid/voided status back in WorkCraft AI only after checking whether a Stripe payment or local invoice already exists. Show sync status, last successful sync, error details, retry, and a disconnect control. Do not duplicate historical invoices by default. Ship Xero through the same internal connector interface after QuickBooks behavior is stable.

**Prerequisite in this app:** Today's `jobs` row carries `invoice_status` and `quoted_total`, and customer payments are linked to an accepted `estimate` rather than a standalone invoice record. Before exporting accounting invoices, introduce a durable invoice identity and snapshot of its line items, tax, total, customer, issue date, and payment allocation. The snapshot must distinguish deposits, remaining-balance payments, partial refunds, and manual cash payments; otherwise the accounting system could receive a different amount or a duplicate payment. Never export the contractor's **WorkCraft AI Pro subscription** as a customer job payment.

**Two-way rules:** Contacts may sync in both directions, with explicit field ownership and a visible conflict review when both sides change. WorkCraft AI invoices flow outward; accounting-side status, payment, and void events flow inward only after reconciliation. Do not let an accounting edit silently rewrite an estimate accepted by a homeowner. Map sales-tax codes and income accounts during setup; let the contractor or bookkeeper verify them rather than inferring tax treatment. Store provider IDs per contractor and object, use idempotent upserts, and prevent an inbound update from being echoed back indefinitely.

**Reliability and security:** Encrypt and rotate OAuth tokens; request the narrowest available scopes; isolate each contractor's accounting connection with row-level security. Verify inbound webhook signatures, acknowledge promptly, and process through a retryable queue. Reconcile with periodic change polling because webhooks can be delayed or missed. Rate-limit exports, surface disconnected or expired authorization, and keep an audit trail of mappings, failures, manual resolutions, and amounts. Test duplicate events, out-of-order updates, a refund after payment, a contact merge, and reconnection to a different company before release.

**Suggested order:** (1) reliable CSV export of customers, invoices, and payments for pilot feedback; (2) one-way QuickBooks Online sync; (3) bounded two-way contact and payment-status reconciliation; (4) Xero connector. Only add deeper two-way invoice editing if pilot users demonstrate a clear need and conflict handling can be made safe.

References: [Intuit's QuickBooks Online webhook guidance](https://static.developer.intuit.com/output_html/qbo/docs/develop/webhooks/best-practices.html), [Xero's webhook overview](https://developer.xero.com/documentation/guides/webhooks/overview), and [Jobber's QuickBooks integration choices](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/how-to-connect-jobber-and-quickbooks-online-new-quickbooks-integration/).
