Use job photos and voice notes with care

A few well-chosen photos or a short voice note can help you remember site conditions. Capture only what helps with the job and treat customer media as private business information.

WWorkCraft AI Field GuideOctober 10, 2026

Field records can save a return visit, but they can also capture information a customer never intended to share.

Take photos that answer a question

Photograph the condition, measurement, or access point you need to remember. Keep the image focused. A small set of clear photos is easier to review than a large collection with no labels or purpose.

Ask before capturing sensitive areas

Get permission when appropriate, especially inside an occupied home or when other people are present. Avoid capturing faces, personal documents, security systems, valuables, children, or unrelated rooms unless there is a clear, authorized reason.

Keep notes factual and job-related

Record measurements, materials, access details, or questions to confirm. A voice note is a memory aid, not a substitute for written customer approval, safety documentation, or a signed change order. Review it before using it in an estimate.

Share only what belongs in the proposal

Private field media may help you prepare the work, but the customer-facing estimate should include only images and details that are relevant and appropriate to share. Explain the scope in words so the estimate is understandable without private notes.

Set a retention habit

Keep records only as long as your business needs and legal obligations require. Delete duplicates and unrelated images, restrict account access, and follow your storage provider’s security and backup guidance.

A privacy-minded field checklist

  • The customer understands why media is being captured.
  • Images avoid unrelated people and private information.
  • Voice notes are reviewed before they inform a proposal.
  • Only appropriate media is shared with the customer.

Privacy, recording-consent, and retention laws vary. Follow the rules where you work and get permission before recording when required.
