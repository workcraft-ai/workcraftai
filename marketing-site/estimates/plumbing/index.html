Before you share

Details to confirm before sending

For repairs behind walls or under slabs, describe what is known and how additional work will be handled before it begins. Keep optional replacements separate from the required repair. Common questions

Separate confirmed work from unknown conditions

Should a plumbing quote include a diagnostic fee?

If you charge one, show it as a separate item and state what it covers. Use your own policy and rates.

How should I describe concealed damage?

Note what could not be inspected, set a clear approval process for added work, and avoid presenting an allowance as a guaranteed final cost.
