Before you share

Details to confirm before sending

A line item such as “electrical labor” leaves room for confusion. Name the device or circuit, its location, and the finish work included. Keep uncertain conditions visible and get customer approval before adding scope. WorkCraft AI lets electricians assemble estimate items, reuse their own price book, and share a review link.

Use the electrical estimate checklist
  • Confirm device locations, quantities, circuit requirements, and the equipment being served.
  • Check access, existing wiring condition, panel capacity, routing, and finish repair boundaries.
  • Identify permit, inspection, outage, and customer-provided fixture assumptions.
  • State testing, labeling, cleanup, and the process for handling concealed or code-required work.

Common questions

Describe the work in customer language

Should material and installation labor be separate?

Separate them when it makes your pricing or customer choices clearer; otherwise describe what the combined line includes.

Do the examples include permit costs?

No. Permit and inspection requirements vary by location and project. Verify local requirements and add your own applicable costs.
