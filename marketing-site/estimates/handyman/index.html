Before you share

Details to confirm before sending

A concise task list can prevent disagreements about what a visit covers. Describe each repair, location, finish level, and material assumption. If the customer adds tasks or hidden damage appears, pause and agree on the revised scope first. WorkCraft AI makes it easier to reuse common items and send the estimate link for review.

Learn how to write a clear contractor estimate
  • List each task and location; identify which rooms or items are excluded.
  • Confirm customer-supplied materials, hardware, finish matching, and access to the work area.
  • State minimum visit charges, included time, disposal, and return-visit assumptions if applicable.
  • Identify hidden damage, permit needs, and work that must be referred to a licensed specialist.

Common questions

Prevent a small repair list from becoming a surprise

Should I estimate a group of small repairs as one job?

You can group them under one proposal, while keeping separate line items for tasks the customer may want to approve or decline individually.

Can a handyman quote licensed electrical, plumbing, or HVAC work?

Follow your local licensing and permit rules. Refer regulated work to a qualified licensed professional where required.
