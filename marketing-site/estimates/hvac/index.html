Before you share

Details to confirm before sending

When equipment selection or site conditions are still being confirmed, label assumptions clearly. Show optional efficiency or control upgrades separately so the customer can compare them. WorkCraft AI helps HVAC contractors turn those choices into organized estimate items and a shareable proposal.

Read the guide to pricing a small trade job
  • Verify equipment specifications, electrical service, duct condition, clearances, and access.
  • State what sizing, load, or site information still needs confirmation before equipment ordering.
  • Clarify included accessories, permits, condensate or vent changes, disposal, and restoration work.
  • Describe startup checks, customer handoff, schedule assumptions, and how changes are approved.

Common questions

Keep allowances and options transparent

Should I put several system options in one line item?

Separate equipment packages or upgrades when the customer needs to compare them; describe each option and its included scope.

Does WorkCraft AI calculate HVAC sizing or local pricing?

No. The examples organize estimate scope. Qualified contractors must determine system design, code requirements, and their own prices.
